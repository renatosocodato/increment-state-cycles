# Sensitivity to ambiguities flagged at the phase boundary

This is a sensitivity of the matched-support genotype × phase contrast to exclusions flagged in the earlier inspection of frames 58–61. It is separate from the masked review of complete traces. The screening scenarios remove eight tracks with unresolved distal signal or a marked tip displacement, or remove those eight plus five tracks with near neighboring tips. The scenarios are diagnostic and use the original model-free q1 estimates. They do not assign corrected biological identities.

At 1 minute and a pause threshold of 0.10 source units, the reference interaction was +0.154 (4 control, 5 CKO). Both screens retained 3 control and 4 CKO animals. In those animals, keeping all eligible tracks produced −0.024. Screening then gave −0.019 (19 retained tracks) and +0.020 (14 retained tracks). The changes attributable to the track removal within the same animals were +0.006 [−0.023, +0.040] and +0.045 [−0.033, +0.165]. These are pointwise percentile bootstrap intervals from 5,000 resamples within genotype, pairing analysis variants within each animal.

The tables report all 18 threshold and sampling combinations. They contain aggregate results only. The EPJ E article and software release are separate from this analysis.

## Masked review of complete tracked sequences

The visual screen examined every available tracked position for the same 27 prolongations across nine movies, including observations outside the analytical windows. A single reviewer inspected ordered tip-centred crops with the direction to the tracked soma marked, using randomized codes and orientations in time. Genotype, nominal phase, and q1 were concealed. The reviewer had seen the boundary frames previously. Image content could provide clues to phase or field identity. Persistent loss of a distinguishable distal signal or competing structures were grounds for ambiguity. The review flagged 21 trajectories and retained six at the 1- and 2-minute sampling intervals. This is a conservative sensitivity screen on the deposited tracking, without independent retracking or E/P/R annotation.

At 1 minute and a pause threshold of 0.10 source units, the genotype × phase interaction in radial q1 was +0.154 with all tracks (4 control, 5 CKO), +0.369 with all tracks in animals retained after screening (3 control, 2 CKO), and +0.074 after screening those same animals (95% interval −0.615 to +0.763). The within-animal change attributable to the exclusion was −0.294 (95% interval −0.875 to +0.287). Changing the set of animals shifted the estimate by +0.215 before any track was removed from a retained animal. The screened contrast took both signs across tested pause thresholds at 1 and 2 minutes. At 4 minutes, the screened set had no eligible CKO animals and the interaction is undefined.

[Complete-trace estimates](complete_trace_sensitivity.csv) and [track counts](complete_trace_counts.csv) cover the six pause thresholds and three sampling intervals. The pointwise intervals resample animals within genotype with 5,000 draws, pairing the screened and unscreened estimates within each retained animal for their difference. The small retained sample makes these intervals descriptive. The contrasts do not account for uncertainty in the visual labels. The full-sequence screen asks a stricter question than the boundary-only screen above.
